\section*{Research Interests}

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Chicago, advised by Victor Veitch and Ari Holtzman. My research is about understanding how Large Language Models (LLMs) reach decisions, and how this informs agentic safety evaluations; hence my research lies at the intersection of causality, interpretability, and game theory.
